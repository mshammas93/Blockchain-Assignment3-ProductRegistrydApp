Product Registry dApp

MG3012 Assignment 3: deployed with Truffle on a local Ganache blockchain

About this dApp

This is the Product Registry from Assignment 2, now compiled and deployed with Truffle to a local Ganache blockchain. A second contract, ProductReviews, lets customers give products a 1 to 5 star rating.

How to use it

  1. Make sure Ganache is running and you ran truffle migrate.
  2. Click Connect MetaMask. It will switch to the Ganache network.
  3. Add products, update prices, and review products using another account.
